Inahitaji Kijiji Kulea Mtoto

It Takes a Village to Raise a Child

A new culturally responsive, community-led support program for Black/African and newcomer families in Hamilton who are navigating or engaged with the child-welfare system.

Stronger families. Connected communities. Children who flourish.

Your family is part of a village.

You do not have to navigate the journey alone. This initiative is designed around listening, cultural understanding, practical connection and family resilience.

Your culture matters. Your family matters. Your community matters.

You don’t have to navigate it alone

For a family, involvement with the child-welfare system can bring uncertainty, difficult conversations, unfamiliar processes and questions about where to find help.

For Black/African and newcomer families, these experiences may also intersect with differences in culture, language, family structures, community expectations and lived experiences.

Inahitaji Kijiji Kulea Mtoto creates a bridge between families, community and available supports.

Our village approach

Our approach is built on three interconnected and culturally responsive pillars.

Family Resilience Circles

Strengthening families through collective support, healing and culturally grounded practices that build connection and resilience.

Community Resource Navigation

Helping families connect with the services, supports, resources and opportunities they need to flourish.

Community Education & Advocacy

Promoting awareness, equity and systemic change within child-welfare and community systems.

Who is this program for?

This program is being developed with our community in mind. The primary audience is Black/African and newcomer families in Hamilton who are navigating the child-welfare system.

Black/African families

Families in Hamilton who are currently engaging with the child-welfare system and may benefit from culturally responsive community support.

Newcomer families

Parents and caregivers who may be navigating both a new country and an unfamiliar child- and family-support system.

Parents & caregivers

Families seeking information, connection, culturally grounded support and easier access to community resources.

Extended family & community

Grandparents, relatives, elders and trusted community members who form part of the village around a child.

Are you a community or service professional?

Community leaders, service providers, family-support professionals and organizations interested in supporting Black/African families are also invited to learn about the initiative and connect with HBHCLF.

Why this matters

Every child deserves the opportunity to flourish without losing connection to family, culture and community.

Representation & Equity

Responding to the overrepresentation and disparity experienced by Black/African children and families within child welfare.

Culturally informed support

Encouraging approaches that recognize culture, identity, lived experience and community.

Family & community resilience

Strengthening the networks around children so families and communities have greater capacity to support them over the long term.

What makes this different?

Community-led

Designed around the strengths, realities and voices of the community.

Culturally responsive

Recognizing that identity, culture and family structures matter.

Family-centred

Looking beyond individual challenges to the strength and well-being of the whole family.

Wrap-around support

Connecting families to multiple forms of community support rather than treating challenges in isolation.

Navigation & connection

Helping make community resources and support easier to understand and access.

Advocacy for change

Supporting greater equity within the systems that serve Black/African children and families.

It takes a village

Children flourish when the people around them flourish too.

A child needs parents and caregivers. But families also need communities. Communities need resources. And systems need to understand the people they serve.

That is the village we are working to strengthen.
