Hamilton Black Health Community Leaders Forum (HBHCLF) serves as the voice of Hamilton’s African Diaspora, advocating for our community’s unique needs at municipal, provincial, and federal levels. Established on September 14, 2021, and inaugurated on May 28, 2022, HBHCLF is a federally registered, non-partisan, and non-profit organization dedicated to the well-being of Hamilton’s African communities.

Today, we stand in unity and profound concern with the Rwandan community and all of Hamilton’s African Diaspora as we mourn the tragic loss of Erixon Kabera, a respected and beloved member of our community, who was shot and killed by Hamilton police on November 9, 2024. This incident has left our collective communities deeply traumatized, and we call for a thorough and transparent investigation into what transpired.

Our concerns are heightened by conflicting and unclear information shared by police and official sources. Initially, Hamilton Police Service (HPS) reported that a shootout occurred between Erixon and two police officers, painting a picture of aggression inconsistent with the man our community knew him to be. However, more than 24 hours later, the Special Investigations Unit (SIU) stated there was no shootout and that Erixon had not fired a weapon. Further updates suggested that a replica firearm was found at the scene. These inconsistencies have raised critical questions:

  • Did the call that led to police intervention come from someone of another race?
  • Did the police fully assess the situation upon arrival?
  • What necessitated the level of force used?
  • Why was a Taser not an alternative in this scenario?
  • Was Erixon’s treatment influenced by racial stereotypes?

As an African-Canadian employed by the Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) with no criminal record, Erixon Kabera was a law-abiding citizen. His death strikes a profound fear within our community, as it could have been any one of us or our children.

We stand with the Kabera family to express that the initial portrayal of Erixon’s death by the HPS is troubling. We ask for a swift and transparent investigation, including the release of video and bodycam footage, to provide clarity and transparency. Our communities deserve answers that will ease the anxiety and trauma we are experiencing.

We offer our deepest condolences to the Kabera family during this time of irreplaceable loss. May Erixon’s soul rests in peace.

Ike Agbassi
Chairperson, HBHCLF
